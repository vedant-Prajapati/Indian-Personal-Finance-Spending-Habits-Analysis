# 📌 Project Overview

Managing personal finances effectively has become increasingly important due to rising living costs and changing spending patterns.

This project analyzes the **Indian Personal Finance and Spending Habits** dataset to understand income, expenses, savings behavior, and financial health. The project applies **Data Analysis, Statistical Analysis, Machine Learning, SQL, and Power BI** to generate actionable insights and build an interactive business dashboard.

The project demonstrates the complete Data Science lifecycle—from raw data to business intelligence.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Individuals often struggle to understand how different spending categories affect their disposable income and savings.

This project aims to identify:

- Major expense categories
- Income distribution across occupations
- Spending behavior
- Savings opportunities
- Financial health
- Key factors affecting disposable income
- Predictive models for estimating disposable income

The insights generated can help individuals, financial institutions, and policymakers make informed financial decisions.

- Perform comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Clean and preprocess financial data

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Income is the strongest predictor of Disposable Income.
- Rent is the largest recurring expense.
- Entertainment and Eating Out offer the highest savings opportunities.
- Tier-1 cities generally have higher income and expenses.
- Random Forest achieved the highest prediction accuracy.
- Most individuals fall into the Good financial health category.
